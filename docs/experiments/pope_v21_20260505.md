## Interpretation

`eg_label_cd` improved recall by about 6.5 points on all three POPE splits and improved F1 on all three splits. However, it also increased Yes Ratio and FPR, especially on the adversarial split. In this configuration, the method is more permissive rather than more conservative.

The current label-level configuration does not yet satisfy the desired goal of reducing false positives. The most relevant next experiments are:

- lower `alpha0` and/or `k_entropy`;
- compare `neg_type=gray` and `neg_type=blur` against `gaussian`;
- evaluate `label_pos`, `vcd_label_const`, and `sample_majority` against `greedy`;
- add a calibration or risk threshold for label-level prediction if FPR reduction is the primary target.
